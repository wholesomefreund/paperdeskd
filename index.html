Questions

Frequently asked.

Does paperly upload my photos?

No. The image processing and PDF creation happen inside your browser. Your images are not intentionally uploaded to a paperly server.

Can I use the camera on my phone?

Yes. On supported mobile browsers, the Take a photo button opens the device camera. The captured image is optimized immediately to reduce memory usage.

Why is the camera button not shown on my computer?

That's intentional. The camera capture control is available only on smaller mobile screens. Desktop users can upload images or drag and drop them into the converter.

How many images can I add?

There is no artificial page limit in paperly. The practical limit depends on your device's available memory. Images are optimized before being stored to make larger documents easier to handle.

What paper size is the PDF?

PDFs are created using the A4 page format with a small margin around each image.

Can I choose the PDF filename?

Yes. Enter the name you want before pressing Create PDF. paperly automatically adds the .pdf extension.

Can I remove a photo after adding it?

Yes. Every page has its own remove control, and you can continue adding more images afterward.

Will my original camera photo stay huge?

No. paperly converts selected images into a smaller JPEG representation as soon as they are added. This is especially important for modern phone cameras that can create very large files.
