Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What image formats can I use? +
paperly accepts common image formats supported by your browser, including JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP and other standard image types. If your device can display the image in a normal browser, it will generally work here.
Can I combine multiple images into one PDF? +
Yes. You can select multiple images at once or add more images after your first upload. Each image becomes a separate page in the final PDF.
Can I choose my own PDF filename? +
Yes. Once you have added pages, use the Document name field to enter whatever filename you want. paperly automatically adds the .pdf extension.
Does paperly work on phones? +
Yes. The interface is designed mobile-first with large touch-friendly controls. On supported phones, you can also use the Take a photo option to add a page directly from your device camera.
Is the camera available on computers? +
No. The camera option is intentionally hidden on desktop and laptop layouts. Computers use normal file selection or drag-and-drop instead.
Are my images uploaded to a server? +
The PDF is generated directly in your browser using the selected images. This version does not send your images to a custom upload server for conversion.
Can I use paperly without creating an account? +
Yes. There is no account or sign-in requirement in this version. Open the page, add your images, create the PDF, and download it.
What size are the generated PDF pages? +
The generated document uses A4 page dimensions. Images are fitted inside the page while maintaining their original aspect ratio.
